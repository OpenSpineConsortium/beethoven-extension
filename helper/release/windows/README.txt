The Beethoven bridge for Windows

This small program lets Beethoven reach the grid's sign-in from your browser without the
GlobalProtect VPN. It carries Beethoven's encrypted connection to the grid and nothing else: it
cannot read what passes through it, it holds no password or key, it keeps no record of anything,
and it starts no other program. Your browser starts it when Beethoven needs it and stops it when
Beethoven is done.

It was installed for your own Windows account only, in this folder, and needed no administrator
rights. The installer you opened was signed, so Windows could check that it came from Beethoven's
maker before it ran.

To remove it: open Settings, choose Apps, find "Beethoven bridge" in the list and choose Uninstall.
That takes away this folder and the two notes that tell Chrome and Edge where the bridge is.
Nothing else on your computer is touched.
