Beethoven 9.0.0: raw screens for the store images (the site theme), captured 2026-10-01 01:31 UTC

Real captures of the release build (extension/.output/chrome-mv3) in Playwright Chromium
153.0.8010.12, light colour scheme, against the mock portal of the store reviewer's recipe (https://localhost:8443,
--user st1234, MOCK_LOGGED_IN=false, the mock's default shared copy and display home /home/st1234). The approval check went to
the mock's access stub, not the live service; the harness held that one request until the strip at "Checking your access..."
was captured, then sent it on. Nothing is drawn or composited. The full screens are 1280x800 PNGs (CSS pixels); the crops are
the elements themselves at device scale 2 (the strip's "-panel" crops with the page narrowed to the side panel's 360 px). The
strip was opened as a tab (the side panel was disabled for the run), so no browser chrome is in any image.

Notes
- 01-signing-dialog-crop.png ends at the summary ("In short"): the first lines of the full text below it carry the
  licensor's copyright line, a personal name, so the crop leaves them out. 01-signing-dialog.png does not reach them.
- 02: the name box shows its focus ring (the name was just typed into it). 04: the code box has focus, as the strip
  gives it when the sign-in page has opened.
- 03: the dot before "Beethoven" pulses while a step is busy; each image catches it at one point of the pulse.
- 04, 05: the box at the bottom of the strip is the output of the last command the strip ran (the check job), with
  paths in the mock's home (/home/st1234/...) as the tool prints them.
- 04: the authorize link in the terminal is the mock's (client_id=mock-client); 06 check 13 shows the mock's
  stand-in sign-in, "student@example.edu max". Neither is anyone's account.
- Times: "last run" and "the service last answered" are this machine's local time (US Eastern), as the extension
  shows them; "checked ... UTC" is UTC.
- The "-panel" crops are the same page rendered 360 px wide; in Chrome the side panel can be dragged wider or
  narrower. At 320 px nothing on these pages runs off the side (measured in a second run of the same recipe).
- The green button is the site's teal (#0E5F58), the colour of the site's selected pill.
