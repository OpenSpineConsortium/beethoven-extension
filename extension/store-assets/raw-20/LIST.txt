Notes
- Real captures of the 20.0.0 build (main at 0c9daaa, extension/.output/chrome-mv3) in Playwright's Chromium 141.0.7390.37,
  headless, light scheme, the fonts loaded (Newsreader, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono), against the mock portal served as
  https://ondemand.grid.wayne.edu (the mock on 127.0.0.1:443, the browser's resolver mapping that host to it, --no-proxy-server;
  nothing reached the university, Anthropic or the lab's site). Mock account st1234; the password was typed into the sheet
  and appears nowhere. Nothing is drawn or composited.
- The package is a scratchpad copy (ext-141) with two changes Chromium 141 needs, since 20.0.0 is built for Chrome 148:
  manifest.minimum_chrome_version 148 -> 141 (Chromium refuses to load it otherwise), and one wrapper on the background's
  runtime.onMessage listener that hands a Promise answer to the callback (background.js.shim.diff; Chromium 141 drops a
  promise-returned answer, so every action's result was undefined and the sheet's sign-in never armed). Nothing visible changes.
- The portal's site permission was granted by writing the origin into the profile's Preferences between two launches
  (headless Chromium shows no permission bubble); the sheet's Remember tick is offered because the panel tab had a CDP virtual
  platform authenticator.
- The panel screens are sidepanel.html opened as a tab at 1280x800, as the 9.0.0 raw screens were; the "-panel" files are
  the same screens at the side panel's own 360x658.
- 02 and 05 carry the folded licence text in their DOM (it names localhost and hpcworker); none of it is on screen.
- The home's "Renews itself at 8:04 AM" is the container's local clock (UTC).
