Ashley Bridge 5.0.0: store assets

Brought up to date 2026-09-29 for 5.0.0. Three screenshots were taken again, from the 5.0.0 release build, between
22:23 and 22:24 UTC: 02, 04b and 05, the only ones whose content changed (see "Which images changed" below). The other
six are the 4.0.0 images of 19:03 to 19:08 UTC, kept because what they show is the same in 5.0.0.

How the 5.0.0 images were made (run D2, e2e/run-D2-own-copy-reviewer-recipe-st1234.txt): the real release build
(extension/.output/chrome-mv3, the 5.0.0 build in the store zips, framework 5 bundled) loaded into Playwright's
Chromium (Chrome for Testing 153) with a fresh profile, a 1280x800 viewport and the light colour scheme, driven
against the mock Open OnDemand portal (extension/mock-ood/server.mjs) served over https at localhost:8443 with a
self-signed certificate, exactly as the store reviewer's recipe runs it: --user st1234 and MOCK_LOGGED_IN=false, the
mock's own home seeding (since 5.0.0 the mock's command line sets its stand-in tool up as the account's own copy at the
version the build carries, so the strip finds the tool installed and uploads nothing; Details reads "tool: your copy,
version 5"). There is no key: the extension read the account name the mock's dashboard shows and asked the real
access service, which answered approved:true for st1234. The agreement was accepted on the setup page as a person
does (scrolled to the end, four boxes ticked, a typed name, "Accept and Continue"). The toolbar button was pressed on
the portal tab with the DevTools protocol's Extensions.triggerAction (the action a click runs), and the browser's own
permission prompt was answered with Windows UI Automation (its Allow button); no extension state was edited. Before
that click the side panel was switched off with chrome.sidePanel.setOptions, so the background's own fallback opened
the strip as a tab, which is what is captured at 1280x800; in Chrome and Edge a person sees the same strip in the side
panel beside the portal. Nothing in the images was drawn by hand or composited. Each image was looked at, and the
harness checked each page's text: no machine path and no personal name appears in any of them.

Caveats
- The strip is captured as a tab, not in the side panel (see above); the content is the same page.
- The agreement text inside the dialog names the licensor (the copyright line and the parties clause of
  LICENSE.txt, the first lines of PRIVACY.md); neither is inside the visible part of 01 or 01b.
- 02, 04b: "Connected - phone - HEAD" and "student@example.edu" are the mock's stand-in Remote Control and
  Claude sign-in values, not the reviewer's or anyone's account.
- 04, 03: the claude.ai tabs the extension opened during the runs (the mock's sign-in URL with a mock client id,
  and the mock's environment link) loaded claude.ai itself; the harness stubbed their assets only (the e2e logs
  say which requests). They are not in any image.
- The time stamps in 02, 04b and 05 are this machine's local time (US Eastern) as the extension shows them; the
  "checked ... UTC" in check 1 and the access card is UTC.
- Run C sent the real access service one check with the portal name "ondemand.wayne.localhost" for st1234 (the
  store reviewer's mock username); the service keys its answer on the account name only.
